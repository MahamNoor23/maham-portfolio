About Me

Frontend Developer who loves building with WordPress.

I'm Maham Noor, a Frontend and WordPress Developer based in Karachi, Pakistan.

I have 2.5+ years of professional development experience working with WordPress, frontend technologies, page builders, custom blocks, WooCommerce, and responsive web interfaces.

My work focuses on turning designs and requirements into clean, responsive and functional websites. I work with both visual builders such as Elementor and Webflow and custom development using HTML, CSS, JavaScript, PHP and WordPress.

I particularly enjoy working with Gutenberg and ACF to create reusable custom blocks and flexible WordPress experiences instead of relying only on off-the-shelf solutions.
